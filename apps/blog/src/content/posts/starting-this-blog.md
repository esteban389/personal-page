I am starting this blog as a place to make technical work easier to revisit and
share.

Some posts will be **technical articles**: structured explanations of a problem,
the options I considered, the implementation, and the tradeoffs behind the final
decision.

Others will be **notes**: shorter observations from debugging, reading,
experiments, or tools I want to remember without turning every idea into a large
essay.

The common thread is simple: write down what was learned while the evidence and
reasoning are still fresh.
